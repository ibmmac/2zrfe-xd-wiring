# Exhaust (same source post)

The xD manifold exit is larger than the Yaris downpipe. It will not bolt to the stock Yaris collector flange.

## Stock manifold or aftermarket direct-fit manifold

1. Cut the Yaris flange off and weld on a larger pivot flange. Downside: Yaris tubing is smaller than the xD, so it is a restriction.
2. Fit an xD midpipe and muffler. OEM is expensive; aftermarket exists.
3. Take it to a local exhaust shop and have a custom piece made.

## RPM long-tube header

1. Will not bolt to any OEM midpipe. Use RPM midpipe or a custom one.
2. Extend the second O2-sensor wires. The long tube moves the bung aft.
3. Pricey. Author called RPM service / shipping hit-and-miss.
4. He ran the RPM header + midpipe. Liked the result. RPM midpipe uses a **flat** rear flange, not the OEM pivot flange. They sent a gasket and it sealed.
5. Hatchback: the xD muffler bolts to the RPM system on the same hangers.
6. DC Sports muffler: extra hanger into a spare-tire-well drain hole. Worked.

## Easiest clean option

If the state allows a used converter (or you can buy aftermarket), staying on the OEM-style xD midpipe + cat is the smoothest path.
